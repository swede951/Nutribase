Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

You can create an account from the login screen by tapping "Sign Up". You'll need to provide an email address and create a secure password. After verifying your email, you can start tracking your nutrition.

Do I need an account to use NutriBase?

No! You can use NutriBase without creating an account. However, creating an account enables cloud sync across devices and backs up your data.

How do I connect to Apple Health?

During the onboarding process, you'll be asked to grant Apple Health permissions. You can also enable/disable specific permissions later in iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > NutriBase.

How do I log food?

Tap the Food tab, select a meal (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snacks), then tap the + button. You can search for foods, scan barcodes, or create custom foods.

How do I customize my dashboard?

From the Dashboard tab, long-press on any card to enter edit mode. You can then drag cards to rearrange them, or tap the minus button to remove cards. Add new cards from the "Available Widgets" section at the bottom.

What is NOVA classification?

NOVA is a food classification system that groups foods by their level of processing. NutriBase tracks NOVA 4 foods (ultra-processed foods) to help you make informed choices about food quality, not just quantity.

How are my nutrition goals calculated?

Your goals are calculated based on your profile (age, gender, height, weight, activity level) and selected nutrition plan. We use evidence-based formulas to estimate your calorie and macro needs. You can always customize these targets manually.

Can I track weight without Apple Health?

Yes! You can manually log weight entries directly in the Weight tab, even without connecting to Apple Health.

How do I change my nutrition goals?

Go to Settings > Profile and adjust your calorie goal, protein target, carb target, and fat target. Changes take effect immediately.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Your data is stored locally on your device by default. If you create an account, synced data is encrypted and stored securely. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will permanently remove all your data from our servers. Local data will remain on your device until you uninstall the app.

The nutrition data seems incorrect. What should I do?

Nutrition data comes from public databases and may occasionally be inaccurate. You can edit any food entry to correct the values, or create a custom food with the correct information.

Can I export my data?

Yes! Contact us at nutribase951@gmail.com to request a data export. We'll provide your data in CSV or JSON format within 7 business days.

Troubleshooting

App is crashing or not responding

  • Try force-quitting the app and reopening it
  • Make sure you're running the latest version of the app
  • Restart your device
  • If the issue persists, contact support with details about when the crash occurs

Data not syncing

  • Ensure you're signed in to your account
  • Check your internet connection
  • Try signing out and signing back in
  • Make sure you're using the same account on all devices

Apple Health data not updating

  • Check that you've granted NutriBase permission in iOS Settings > Health
  • Try toggling the permissions off and back on
  • Ensure Apple Health itself is working properly
  • Some data may take a few minutes to sync

Barcode scanner not working

  • Ensure you've granted camera permissions in iOS Settings > NutriBase
  • Make sure there's adequate lighting
  • Try cleaning your camera lens
  • Not all barcodes are in our database - you can manually add the food
