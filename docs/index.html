Science-backed scoring
Three lenses on every food

We layer the world's best food quality frameworks so you get a complete picture — not just a calorie number.

NOVA

Ultra-Processing Level

Classifies every food into one of four groups based on its degree of industrial processing. Developed by researchers at the University of São Paulo and used in public health policy worldwide.

A B C

Nutri-Score

The official EU nutrition label grading foods A–E based on positive nutrients (fibre, protein) and negative nutrients (sugar, saturated fat, sodium). Recognised by governments across Europe.

87

Gut Health Score

Our proprietary daily score combining food quality (43%), processing level (33%), and fibre density (23%). Updates in real-time as you log. The more whole, diverse foods you eat, the higher your score.

NOVA Classification
What's actually in your food?

Most apps see "chicken wrap" and count calories. FoodScore sees whether it's homemade or ultra-processed — a distinction that matters enormously for long-term health.

Ultra-processed foods (Group 4) are linked to increased risk of obesity, type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and colorectal cancer. FoodScore tracks your daily UPF percentage so you stay informed.

1

Unprocessed / Minimally Processed

Fruits, vegetables, eggs, plain meat, legumes, nuts

2

Processed Culinary Ingredients

Oils, butter, flour, salt, sugar, honey used in cooking

3

Processed Foods

Canned fish, cheese, cured meats, beer, wine

4

Ultra-Processed Foods

Soft drinks, packaged snacks, chicken nuggets, instant noodles, most breakfast cereals
