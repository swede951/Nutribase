## App Description (4000 char max)

**Character count: ~2,800**

```
NutriBase is your intelligent nutrition companion, designed to help you understand not just how much you eat, but the quality of what you eat.

SMART NUTRITION TRACKING
• Log meals quickly with barcode scanning and a comprehensive food database
• Track calories, protein, carbs, and fat with precision
• Set personalized daily goals based on your body and activity level
• View your nutrition at a glance with a beautiful, customizable dashboard

FOOD QUALITY INSIGHTS WITH NOVA
Unlike other calorie counters, NutriBase helps you understand food quality using the NOVA classification system. Track your intake of ultra-processed foods and make informed choices about what you eat, not just how much.

• See NOVA scores for foods you log
• Monitor your ultra-processed food percentage
• Get insights into food processing levels
• Make better choices with quality-focused tracking

WEIGHT TRACKING & TRENDS
• Log weight manually or sync with Apple Health
• View trends over weeks, months, and years
• Set weight goals with realistic timelines
• Track phases like cutting, bulking, or maintenance
• Smooth trend lines filter out daily fluctuations

APPLE HEALTH INTEGRATION
• Sync weight data automatically
• Import step count and activity calories
• Keep all your health data in one place
• Works seamlessly with your Apple Watch

CUSTOMIZABLE DASHBOARD
• Drag and drop widgets to create your perfect view
• Choose from multiple card sizes and layouts
• Focus on the metrics that matter most to you
• Quick access to daily goals and progress

FLEXIBLE MEAL LOGGING
• Organize foods by Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks
• Save favorite meals for quick logging
• Create custom foods with your own nutrition data
• Copy meals between days
• Detailed nutrition breakdown for every entry

GOAL SETTING & PROGRESS
• Calculate your daily calorie needs automatically
• Set macro targets for protein, carbs, and fat
• Choose from preset nutrition plans or create your own
• Track progress toward your goals with visual indicators

PRIVACY FOCUSED
• Your data stays on your device by default
• Optional cloud sync with secure encryption
• No ads, no selling your data
• You control what you share

Whether you're trying to lose weight, build muscle, or simply eat better, NutriBase gives you the tools and insights to succeed. Start your journey to better nutrition today.

Download NutriBase and take control of your nutrition.
```
